# Target buyers who must hand numbers over

Research date: 2026-10-16 (track M, task M04)

StartupModel should build its first SEO pages for searchers who need to hand their numbers to someone who will check them. That means bank and SBA lenders, seed investors and visa adjudicators, and the advisors who prepare files for them. It should not build first for people searching "template", "free" or "calculator". In the results we observed, the searches that signal "build it for me", such as **"custom financial model"** and **"sba loan financial projections"**, are served by freelance gigs, service landing pages and weak or spammy pages. They are not held by entrenched publishers, so a well-made product page can compete there. The head terms ("financial projections template", "saas financial model", "DCF model template") belong to SCORE, CFI, Smartsheet, Chargebee and Wall Street Oasis, which give the file away for free. Many "[niche] financial model template" searches show template marketplaces, and Henry's separate FinModelsLab store appears there (coffee shop, online grocery and agency queries). StartupModel must stay off those queries. The plan below has **15 pages in three waves of five**. Wave 1 is a custom-model hub, an SBA/bank-loan projections page, restaurant/coffee-shop loan projections (the cafe template exists today), a SaaS model with scenarios, and professional-services projections. **No search volume was measured for any keyword**. The ordering rests on intent and qualitative difficulty only, so it must be re-checked against volumes before M06 starts.

## Method and limits: no volumes, a proxy SERP, qualitative difficulty

The Ahrefs connector returned **"Insufficient plan"**, so **no search volumes and no keyword-difficulty scores were measured**. The "volume" column says "not measured" for every keyword, and no number in this document stands in for a volume. The SERP observations come from a US web-search tool run on 2026-10-16. It is **not live Google**. It returns about ten links in rough rank order, with no position numbers, SERP features, ads or local packs, and no UK results. Several "...template excel" queries returned parasite-spam pages on hijacked subdomains (for example [live.museum.manchester.ac.uk](https://live.museum.manchester.ac.uk/library/sba-financial-projections-template.html) and [chw.dhis2.org](https://chw.dhis2.org/template/rental-property-proforma-template-excel.html)). These were left out of the competitor lists, and the affected queries were re-run in extended mode where possible.

Difficulty is a judgement based on which domains appear. **High** means authority finance, education or portal brands (SCORE, CFI, Smartsheet, Shopify, BiggerPockets). **Med** means established template shops and SaaS blogs. **Low** means gigs, small sellers or spam. A difficulty marked `*` is inferred from a neighbouring query, because that exact SERP was not run. Before any wave is re-prioritised, someone must pull volumes from Ahrefs or Semrush on a sufficient plan, or from Google Keyword Planner, which needs Henry's Google Ads account. That is a Henry item for docs/HUMAN_TODO.md.

## Notes for the M05 and M06 writers

Every page needs a **downloadable sample `.xlsx` generated by the engine**, never one built by hand. The sample must have passed the challenger and have no FAIL on its Checks sheet. Name the sample's template, tier and module in the page front matter (`template:`). If the template is not merged yet, swap the page for a ready one rather than shipping a page with no sample. Write as an experienced CFO explaining what a lender, investor or plan writer will check. Base each page on official sources, quoted accurately. Loan pages (2, 3, 6, 8, 9, 13) cite the SBA business-plan page's request for a five-year outlook with forecasted income statement, balance sheet and cash flow, monthly or quarterly in year 1 ([SBA](https://legacy.sba.gov/business-guide/plan-your-business/write-your-business-plan)). The startup-costs angle can cite [SBA's startup-costs page](https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/plan-your-business/calculate-your-startup-costs?utm_gp=1). Page 10 cites [9 FAM 402.9](https://fam.state.gov/fam/09FAM/09FAM040209.html#M402_9_6_B). No page may say "SBA-approved", "lender-approved" or "visa-approved".

Do not invent statistics, approval rates, user counts or testimonials. If a number has no source link, leave it out, and keep prices as `[PRICE]` placeholders unless Henry has set them. Never copy or paraphrase FinModelsLab text, never link to FinModelsLab, and never mention it. The same rule applies to getdraftplan.com's name and copy. Keep "template" out of titles and H1s except on guide pages 6, 7 and 9, and even there the call to action is "built on your numbers", not "download a template". Each page links up to hub page 1, and each loan page also links to page 2. Every page should state the proof points in plain words: live formulas, a Low/Base/High switch, a Checks sheet, and an independent recompute that matches every scenario-year within $1.

## Conclusion

This plan does not try to win the head terms. It targets searchers who face a deadline set by a third party, which is where our ability to prove the numbers matters most and where FinModelsLab does not rank. That choice also lowers the cost of the missing volume data. Even if the loan and "custom" searches are small, each visitor is close to buying, and the head terms we skip would bring mostly $0 template seekers. The main uncertainty is size, not fit. The next M-track step should be a volume pull for these 15 primary keywords, together with a check of the untested long-tail SERPs (rental for lenders, retail for a bank loan, the DCF owner angle, the accountant B2B angle). The waves should be re-ordered only if that data shows a wave-1 primary has no measurable demand.
