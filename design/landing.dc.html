From brief to workbook in three steps

  1. Step 1

    Answer the brief

    Plain questions about how you sell, what it costs and what you need to decide. No finance terms. Not ready to share numbers? Start from sample data and change it later.

  2. Step 2

    We build it, then check it

    Your answers become a structured brief. Tested templates write every formula. A separate program then rebuilds every total from your inputs and compares, in all three cases.

  3. Step 3

    Download and make it yours

    Open it in Excel. Change any input and every sheet follows. The file is yours to keep, edit and send to your lender or investor.

We check the maths before you see it

After the workbook is built, an independent program recalculates it from your inputs. If any annual total differs by more than one dollar, or any check fails, the file is held back and fixed. The same checks stay in your workbook as live formulas, so they keep working after you edit it.

  • ✓The case switch resolves low, base and high
  • ✓No error values anywhere in the file
  • ✓A funding gap is flagged if cash goes below zero

Questions

Is it a real Excel file?
Yes. A standard .xlsx with working formulas across sheets, not a chat answer or a screenshot. It uses only long-established Excel functions.
Do I need a finance background?
No. The questions are in plain language, and you can start from sample data and replace it with your numbers later.
How is it different from a template?
A template is the same file for everyone. Your workbook is built from your answers, so the line items, drivers and cases already match how your business earns and spends.
What exactly is checked?
Totals, cash roll-forward, balance sheet, the case switch and error values. An independent recalculation must agree with the workbook to the dollar before it is sent.
