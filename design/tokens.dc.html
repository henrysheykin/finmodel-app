Layout

  • Container max 1180 px, side padding 24 px (16 px under 480 px)
  • Sections 88 px top and bottom (56 px on phones), left-aligned headings
  • Structure from rules and tables, not cards: 2 px ink rule over groups, 1 px rule between rows
  • One dark section only: the checks

Motion and voice

  • No scroll-reveal animations. Only responses to the visitor: case switch and sheet tabs update instantly
  • Sentence case everywhere, no all-caps labels, no arrows in buttons
  • Plain verbs: Start a model, Choose Full, Download .xlsx
  • No invented numbers: prices and timings stay placeholders until decided
