We want you to keep a model only if it is useful to you.

# 7-day refund

If the model does not fit your business, write to [support@startup-model.com](mailto:support@startup-model.com) within 7 days of payment, from the email address you ordered with, and include your order number if you have it. We refund the full price; you do not have to give a reason, although a line on what was missing helps us improve.

Refunds go back to the card or payment method you used, through Stripe. We start the refund within 2 business days of your request; your bank usually shows it within 5–10 business days.

After a refund, please delete your copies of the model; the licence in our [Terms of service](/terms) ends with the refund.

# If we cannot deliver

If your model does not pass our checks, we do not send it. If we cannot deliver a model that passes them, we refund you in full.

# Duplicate payments

If you are charged twice for the same order, the second payment is refunded automatically.

# After 7 days

After 7 days from payment we do not refund for a change of mind. If something is wrong with the file itself — for example a formula error or a check that fails — write to us at any time and we will fix it or, if we cannot, refund you.

# Your legal rights

This policy is in addition to your rights by law. If you are a consumer in the European Union, the law gives you rights regarding digital content, including when it is faulty, and this policy does not limit them.
