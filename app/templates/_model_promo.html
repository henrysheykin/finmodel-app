A calculator answers one question with one set of numbers. A real decision rarely stays that small: a higher price changes how many customers come, more customers change the hours you staff, and rent, loan repayments and tax all have to be paid out of what is left. A full model keeps those links in one workbook, so you can change an assumption and see the profit, the cash balance and the break-even point move together.

That is also what a lender, an investor or a partner will ask to see: the assumptions in one place, a low, a base and a high case, and proof that the arithmetic holds. You answer a short brief and get an Excel file with live formulas and a Checks sheet. Every scenario is recomputed independently before delivery.
